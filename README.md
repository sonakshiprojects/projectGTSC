# Supply Chain Analytics & Inventory Optimization

## Problem Statement

Efficient inventory management is critical to maintaining product availability while avoiding excess inventory and unnecessary holding costs. This project analyzes supply chain data to identify demand patterns, inventory movement, stockout risks, and supplier performance, enabling data-driven inventory planning and operational improvements.

## Project Overview

An end-to-end supply chain analytics project focused on **inventory optimization, demand analysis, stockout risk, and supplier performance** using Python, MySQL, and MS Excel.

## Key Objectives

- Analyze 50K+ supply chain transactions to identify inventory and demand patterns.
- Evaluate inventory levels and classify products based on their movement and demand.
- Identify products with higher **stockout risk** and support safety stock planning.
- Evaluate supplier performance and identify operational improvement opportunities.
- Develop actionable insights to support data-driven inventory decisions.

## Key Analysis

- Processed **50K+ transactions** using Python and MySQL to identify inventory movement, demand patterns, supplier performance, and operational trends.
- Performed **inventory classification, demand analysis, and safety stock analysis** using Python and MS Excel to identify stockout risks and support inventory planning.
- Applied **quantitative analysis and KPI evaluation** to assess inventory and supplier performance and generate actionable recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Excel:** Pivot Tables, KPI Analysis, Data Analysis
- **Analytics:** Inventory Classification, Demand Analysis, Safety Stock Analysis, Stockout Risk Analysis, Supplier Performance Analysis

## Business Insights

The analysis provides visibility into **inventory movement, demand trends, stockout risk, and supplier performance**, helping support better inventory planning, product availability, and overall supply chain efficiency.
